# Phishing Awareness

## What is Phishing?
Phishing is a social engineering attack designed to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, financial information, or login credentials.

Attackers often impersonate trusted organizations or individuals.

---

## Common Signs of Phishing Emails
- Urgent or threatening language
- Suspicious links
- Misspelled words or grammar issues
- Requests for passwords or sensitive information
- Unknown senders
- Fake login pages
- Unexpected attachments

---

## Types of Phishing
### Email Phishing
Mass phishing emails sent to many users.

### Spear Phishing
Targeted phishing attacks focused on specific individuals or organizations.

### Smishing
Phishing attacks sent through SMS/text messages.

### Vishing
Voice phishing attacks conducted through phone calls.

---

## Phishing Prevention Best Practices
- Verify sender email addresses
- Avoid clicking suspicious links
- Use MFA whenever possible
- Keep software updated
- Report suspicious emails
- Verify requests through trusted communication channels

---

## Why Phishing is Dangerous
Phishing attacks can lead to:
- Credential theft
- Financial loss
- Malware infections
- Data breaches
- Unauthorized system access
