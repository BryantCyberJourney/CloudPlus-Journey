# Incident Response Basics

## What is Incident Response?
Incident response is the process of identifying, managing, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

The goal is to minimize damage and restore normal operations quickly.

---

## Common Cybersecurity Incidents
- Malware infections
- Phishing attacks
- Data breaches
- Unauthorized access
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Ransomware attacks

---

## Incident Response Lifecycle

### Preparation
Establish policies, tools, backups, and response procedures.

### Identification
Detect and confirm security incidents.

### Containment
Limit the spread and impact of the incident.

### Eradication
Remove malicious activity or compromised systems.

### Recovery
Restore systems and return to normal operations.

### Lessons Learned
Review the incident and improve future security processes.

---

## Important Incident Response Concepts
- Documentation is critical
- Preserve evidence when possible
- Communicate clearly during incidents
- Follow security procedures
- Perform post-incident analysis
